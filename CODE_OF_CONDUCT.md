# Code of Conduct

Keyway is open to everyone. We want issues, reviews and discussions to stay respectful and useful.

## What we expect

- Be kind. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it constructively.
- Keep discussion on topic, and keep other people's private details private.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sharing someone's personal information, and spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, which is private, and say it is a conduct report. You will get a reply from a maintainer.

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions.
